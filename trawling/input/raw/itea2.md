
1. KSO — Organisation Description / Profile (ITEA4)

Kleynenberg Software Ontwikkeling (KSO) is a specialized Dutch R&D SME led by Steven E. Kleynenberg (Principal AI & Systems Architect), focusing on sovereign, privacy-by-design Artificial Intelligence, hybrid Enterprise-RAG, and browser-native / edge-AI architectures.
Building on over 25 years of architectural leadership in previous ITEA and EU consortia—including EDAFMIS (ITEA 2 07011), MEDUSA (ITEA 2 10004), Medolution (ITEA 3 14003), and the Award-winning PARTNER project (ITEA 3 16017)—KSO bridges rigorous academic foundations in neural networks and NLP (co-inventor of PCT patent WO 2014/003543 A1) with rapid, first-principles prototyping for modern AI ecosystems.
What KSO brings to ITEA4 AI Consortia:
• Sovereign & Hybrid Enterprise-RAG: Combining deterministic linguistic query engineering (Part-of-Speech tagging, semantic indexing) with dense vector search and local/on-premise LLM pipelines to eliminate hallucination and vendor lock-in.
• Browser-Native & Edge-AI Acceleration: Zero-footprint, client-side AI execution using WebGPU/WebGL compute shaders, local speech-to-text/text-to-speech (gpuspeech), and offline-first Progressive Web Apps (PWAs).
• Real-Time Telemetry, IoT & Clinical Decision Support: End-to-end sensor ingestion (Bluetooth LE biometric wearables, LoRaWAN) paired with rule-based decision support engines (DSS) and interactive 3D/multidimensional visual analytics.
• Agile SME Partner for Exploitation & Demonstrators: Proven track record in translating complex consortium architectures into working software demonstrators, open standards, and commercially viable tools.
