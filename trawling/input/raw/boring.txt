## Architectural Synthesis (1985–2026)

- **Hardware-Agnostic Neural Architectures (1988–2026):** Originated with the **CALM** system and **MetaNet Network Environment** (Leiden University, 1988–1991), which decoupled neural network topology from execution hardware (ANSI C, T800 Transputers, 400-processor BSP400 neurocomputer, Connection Machine). Continued today in browser-native and edge-AI systems including **Synaptix Studio**, **gpuspeech** (WebGPU TTS), and **NeuralNova** (KSA-16).
- **Linguistic Information Retrieval to Hybrid Enterprise-RAG (1996–2026):** Co-inventor of PCT application **WO 2014/003543 A1** (filed 2012, published 2014 at Sopheon N.V.) for Part-of-Speech (POS) query generation and document clustering. Serves as a deterministic lexical pre-filtering foundation for modern hybrid Enterprise-RAG pipelines (**Innopheon2 / Phoenix**, **Granite-VectorAI** / KSA-02).
- **Clinical Decision Support & Real-Time Sensor Telemetry (2000–2026):** Extensive architectural involvement in European Commission and ITEA medical HPC/cloud consortia at Sopheon N.V. (*Edafmis*, *MEDUSA*, *Medolution*, *PARTNER*), bridging wearable sensor data and 3D medical visualization with clinical workflows. Directly mirrored in current R&D projects **Zephyr2026** (Bluetooth LE biometric telemetry for Zephyr BioHarness and Polar H7) and **OrionPulse** (KSA-18).
- **Mission-Critical & Parallel Simulation Systems (1991–1996):** Designed deterministic Model-Based Reasoning (MBR) diagnostics for the Abrams Main Battle Tank (Netherlands Ministry of Defence / Leiden University), real-time TCAS air traffic collision avoidance algorithms/radar interfaces (Civil Aviation Authority), and distributed Cellular Automata traffic simulations (TU Delft / Ministry of Transport).
