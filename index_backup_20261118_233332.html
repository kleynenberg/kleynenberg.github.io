About — first-principles career architecture

01 · The constraint

Decades of primary engineering artifacts, peer-reviewed papers, international patents, and low-level system architectures are routinely flattened into generic, ad-ridden social network templates controlled by third-party algorithms.

02 · Structural cost

When your career record lives inside a centralized database, you surrender ownership of your data, expose visitors to behavioral tracking, and reduce complex technical proofs into superficial resume bullet points that neither humans nor AI agents can deeply inspect.

03 · Architectural standard

A professional identity should act as a sovereign, zero-latency, machine-readable knowledge graph: instantly installable as a standalone app, verifiable down to the source code and patent claims, and effortlessly parsed by modern AI crawlers.

04 · Sovereign implementation

Project UnLinked (https://kleynenberg.github.io) replaces centralized platform dependency with a self-contained static PWA built from first principles — unifying 40 years of verified R&D milestones (1985–2026) with live repositories and autonomous media streams.
